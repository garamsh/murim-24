# Evidence for #179

Captures of the faction window from issue #165, phase 2 (PR #179). The mockup the PM approved is on evidence/pr-177. This branch is never merged.

The images were made by `capture_factions.gd` at e225331, which holds the HUD and play scene unchanged at the PR's head (b013cdc; later commits change only the core's code layout, tests and docs). I ran it with Godot 4.7.2 on macOS arm64 after `make build`: copied to the project root, then `godot --path . --script res://capture_factions.gd -- <dir>`.

Each shot starts from seed 2026's region with five-tick days, lived 10 years. The play scene's own ticks are held, and every key goes through the play scene's `factions()` as F, the arrows, Enter and C do.

Names are `<view>_<run>_<locale>.png`. The runs:
- `pc_1920x1080`: the HUD at 2x.
- `touch_1920x1080`: the HUD at 4x.
- `touch_2400x1080`: the HUD at 4x, centred.

The views:
- `hud`: the HUD. The leader line has no "C 도전", and on touch the 세력 button replaces 도전.
- `leader`: steering the Orthodox leader (army 0, person 0), with the Unorthodox (ally) chosen. War is offered; peace is greyed with "already at peace".
- `confirm`: then Enter. War is asked again.
- `declared`: then Enter again. The core holds the war, declared by the Orthodox leader at Spring, Y11. War is now greyed as "already at war", peace as "too soon", and the basis is shown.
- `member`: steering a Demonic Cult member (army 4, person 8). No orders; only the leader decides; the challenge to the leader is offered.
- `refused`: steering an Orthodox member (army 3, person 6), F then C. The core's `CHALLENGE_NO_SUCCESSION` word is shown in the window.
